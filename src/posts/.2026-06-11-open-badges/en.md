# Digital badges: much more than a pretty picture

If you work in e-learning, you have surely seen them: those medals that show up on Moodle™ profiles, on LinkedIn, or on learning platforms. They are **digital badges**, and they are much more than a pretty picture.

## What is a digital badge?

A digital badge is a **verifiable credential**. Behind the image travels a package of metadata: who issues it, who it is awarded to, what criteria you have to meet to earn it, and when it was issued. Anyone can check its authenticity on the issuer's verification page, without trusting screenshots.

All of this works thanks to [Open Badges](https://openbadges.org/), an open standard maintained by 1EdTech. And since it is a standard, badges are **portable**: you earn them on one platform and you can display them wherever you want.

In Moodle, badges [have been first-class citizens](https://docs.moodle.org/en/Badges) for more than a decade: they can be issued at site or course level, with automatic criteria (completing a course, an activity...) or manual ones. There is a reason we like them so much in this little world: they motivate, they recognise specific achievements, and they tell your learning story better than a line on a CV.

## A badge for learning about badges

Out of my whole collection there is one I am especially fond of: the **attendee badge for the talk "The future of Open Badges"** by [Sara Arjona](https://moodledev.io/general/community/credits#moodle-hq) at MoodleMoot España 2025, at the Universitat de València.

Yes, you read that right: a badge for attending a talk about badges. The ultimate meta-credential \o/

Sara walked us through where the standard is heading: **Open Badges 3.0**, which builds on the [W3C Verifiable Credentials](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) so that the badge carries its cryptographic signature with it and can be verified without depending on the issuer staying online. A huge evolution for something that started as little more than a PNG with metadata. If you are interested, [the presentation was recorded](https://docenciavirtual.uv.es/video/6811ff4f3f3220a43e0198c2) and you can watch it in full.

And of course, [here you can verify my badge](https://moodlemoot.es/badges/badge.php?hash=ea4469715d24ad81c32789dbb77fbda52ae539e9). That is the whole point. 😉
