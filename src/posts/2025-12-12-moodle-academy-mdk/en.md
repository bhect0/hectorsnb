# MDK course at Moodle Academy

[Moodle™ Academy](https://moodle.academy/) is Moodle's official learning platform. I have completed many developer courses since it launched and all of them have been very useful to me.

I can't help highlighting the fact that you learn about Moodle through a Moodle instance. Simply wonderful \o/

## A course about MDK

This month they have published an [excellent course about MDK](https://moodle.academy/enrol/index.php?id=171) (*Moodle Development Kit*). If you don't know MDK, take a look at [its repository](https://github.com/FMCorz/mdk), and you will quickly see how much it helps.

MDK is a command-line tool that automates the repetitive day-to-day tasks of developing for Moodle: creating new instances, installing the database, switching branches, applying patches or running the tests. What you would normally do by hand chaining a bunch of commands, MDK solves in one. It even has a workflow designed specifically for working with the [tracker](https://moodledev.io/general/development/tracker) directly.

## The hands-on part

*(Spoiler alert!)* To finish the course and check whether you have absorbed what you learned, you have to pick an issue from the tracker and carry out the whole fixing process with MDK. The perfect opportunity to contribute to Moodle core for the first time!

If you develop for Moodle and you are not using MDK yet, this course is an unbeatable way to get started.
